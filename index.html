What is this?

A field map of engineering discussion

What you're looking at

A field map of ~3,900 engineering knowledge frames extracted from Stack Exchange discussions across five sites (Engineering, Electronics, Robotics, Mechanics, Woodworking). Each bubble is a topic cluster; each node inside is a discrete claim — a risk, a heuristic, a design principle, a worked case. Lines mean topics share semantically-linked evidence.

Three levels of drill-down

  1. Overview — 39 topic bubbles. Size = frame count. Edges = shared inter-topic semantic links.
  2. Topic — click a bubble to see its frames laid out by similarity. Toggle Force graph in the panel for a live physics view with spiral or natural layouts.
  3. Frame — click a frame to read its source quote, author, and ego-network of connected frames. Use +/− to expand by more hops.

Key vocabulary

Frame
A single extracted claim — a risk, heuristic, case, principle, or observation. ~3,900 total.
Topic
A cluster of frames grouped by BERTopic (unsupervised). 39 topics total.
Semantic edge
A model-inferred relationship between two frames (elaborates, contradicts, provides-evidence-for…). ~4,200 total.
Shared semantic edges (overview slider)
Count of frame-to-frame links crossing between two topic bubbles. The slider hides pairs below the threshold — higher N = sparser, more meaningful skeleton.

Navigation & controls

  • Click any topic or frame to drill in. The breadcrumb (top-left) shows where you are; Back in the panel goes up a level.
  • Scroll to zoom; drag the background to pan. In per-topic force graph view, enable drag from the panel to pin individual frames.
  • FORCE GRAPH toggle (top-right) switches the overview to a live canvas simulation of all frames at once, colored by topic.
  • Search by frame text, topic label, or author. Filter by frame type, site, credibility, or year.
